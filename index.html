    How to Play

    Objective

    The grid holds one Power Source, a set of Appliance nodes and a few Obstacle blocks. Build domino chains out from the source until every appliance is lit. You may only go out (empty your hand) once the whole circuit is powered.

    Placing dominoes
    • A domino covers two adjacent cells, horizontally or vertically.
    • At least one of its halves must sit orthogonally next to a placed tile with the same pip count.
    • Only one matching edge is required — the domino may also touch tiles it does not match.
    • Obstacle blocks and occupied cells can never be used.
    Blanks are wild

    Any half showing 0 is wild: it accepts a neighbour of any value. When you place it, declare the value it displays (or leave it wild). Its wild nature is permanent, so blanks make excellent bridges.

    Doubles are junctions

    A double (0-0, 1-1 … 6-6) has the same count on both halves, so both halves accept the same values and tiles can branch off in several directions. That is how the circuit spreads.

    Turns, drawing and passing
    1. Place one domino, or
    2. If you have no legal move, click Draw Tile. You draw exactly one tile.
    3. If the drawn tile is playable you may play it immediately; otherwise click End Turn.
    4. If the boneyard is empty and you cannot move, you must pass.
    Going out and winning
    • Solo: empty your hand while every appliance is powered. Score = tiles still left in the boneyard. If you empty your hand too early, you must draw one tile.
    • Pass & Play: the first player to go out with the circuit complete ends the level. Everyone else scores the pips left in their hand — a blank counts as 10 penalty points. Lowest cumulative total across levels wins.
    • If the boneyard is empty and nobody can move, the circuit stalls and the level ends.

    Tips
    • Place a horizontal domino by clicking its left cell; a vertical one by clicking its top cell. Rotate to fit tight corners.
    • Green cells when a tile is selected are legal anchors — useful on touch screens.
    • Keep a blank or a double in reserve to jump a pip gap near an appliance.
    • Power flows only through matching pips, so a chain that touches an appliance on the wrong value stays dark.

    Declare blank value

    A blank half is wild and matches any pip. Choose the value it displays on the board, or leave it wild.
